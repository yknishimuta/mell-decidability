# T10-C4-2B audit: strict rational-linear dimension decrease

Baseline for C4-2 was `9f3a1a5755ce5dfc64b8dea0f17a9b67b6b05519`.
C4-2A is preserved by commit `3f70b6662879b80bf5a531db7d2b14d1b723f2f8`.

## Representation and dimension

`QcLinearAlgebra.v` equips Coq's canonical rational type `Qc` with the
MathComp equality, choice, ring, and field structures.  The choice structure
uses an explicit, canceling numerator/denominator code; it does not use a
choice axiom.  `QVec d` is transported to MathComp row vectors by
`qvec_to_qcrow`, with proved preservation/reflection of `QVecEq`, zero,
addition, scaling, and the rational inner product.

`QSpanOf set value` means that `value` belongs to the MathComp span of a
finite list of points of `set`.  A `SpanPresentation set generators` is a
finite list that presents exactly that span.  `SpanDimension set n` means
that such a presentation exists and that `n` is MathComp's subspace
dimension `\dim`; it is not the length of a cone-generator list.

For every cone, `qspan_of_cone_difference` proves pointwise
`QSpanOf cone = cone - cone`.  The forward direction splits arbitrary
rational coefficients into nonnegative positive and negative parts; the
reverse direction uses closure of spans under addition and `-1` scaling.
Finite V-presentations therefore provide finite span presentations, and
`span_dimension_unique`, `span_dimension_monotone`, and
`span_dimension_le_presentation` connect them to the standard finite-space
dimension laws.

## Proper faces

`proper_face_span_strict` extracts a point in the larger face but not the
smaller face.  From the smaller face's finite supporting-normal list it
selects a normal that is nonzero on this witness.  Every generator of the
smaller face's span lies in that normal's linear kernel, while the witness in
the larger span does not.  This establishes strict subspace inclusion without
pointedness, full dimensionality, or generator independence.

`proper_face_dimension_lt` applies MathComp's finite-dimensional subspace
dimension theorems to obtain strict inequality.  The cone-specialized
`proper_face_of_cone_dimension_lt` and the ordering bridge
`linearized_proper_faces_index_and_dimension` give, respectively, strict
decrease for a proper face of the cone and both `i < j` and dimension decrease
for a proper inclusion in a C4-2A list.

## Regression coverage

`FaceDimensionExamples.v` checks dimension zero, the zero face, the exact
dimensions 2/1/0 for the rational nonnegative quadrant/ray/zero face, strict
ray-to-quadrant decrease, and invariance under duplicate and redundant
generators.  It also checks existence of a dimension for a nonpointed cone
with lineality.  A concrete halfspace generated by the two-sided x-axis and
the positive y-axis checks that its lineality face is proper and has strictly
smaller dimension.  A concrete two-entry list `[quadrant; ray]` instantiates
`linearized_proper_faces_index_and_dimension`, checking both `0 < 1` and
`1 < 2`.  C4-2A already checks extensional duplicate removal, exact cone
head, reverse-inclusion order, zero-dimensional ordering, and the four
standard quadrant faces.

The C4-1 bound remains available as
`polyhedral_faces_finite_bounded`: before extensional deduplication and
ordering, the representative list has length at most `2^m`.  The C4-2A
transformations are subsequence/permutation plus insertion of the exact cone,
but the present public `polyhedral_faces_linearized` contract does not expose
the original generator list or a numerical bound.  Packaging the same bound
with the ordered witness is left as an optional C4-3 interface refinement;
no mathematical claim is weakened here.

## Trust boundary and verification

The algebraic bridge, span/difference theorem, existence and uniqueness of
dimension, and basic monotonicity are constructive.  The strict-face results
inherit only `Classical_Prop.classic`, used to obtain a witness from failure
of reverse inclusion.  There is no dependency on `ClassicalEpsilon`, UIP/K,
`Eq_rect_eq`, functional extensionality, `Admitted`, a new `Axiom`, or an
external oracle.

The focused build, `Print Assumptions`, `coqchk -silent`, C4-0/C4-1/C4-2A
regressions, the full Coq build, and independent review are recorded in the
completion report after their final runs.

The final independent delta review approved the implementation with no
critical or important findings.  It separately checked the lineality-face
example, the concrete ordered quadrant/ray bridge, the Q/nat scope boundary,
the `Classical_Prop.classic` dependency, and a silent kernel check.

## C4-3 boundary

C4-3 still must formulate and prove the face-stripping argument itself.  It
may reuse the finite reverse-inclusion list, strict dimension descent, and the
existing finite face bound.  No face-stripping theorem, induction over that
list, or downstream decidability result is proved in C4-2.
