## Public theorem

With `Set Printing Implicit`, the final public type is:

```coq
lattice_gordan
  : forall (d : nat) (group : ZSet d)
      (cone : RationalDefinability.QSet d),
      @FinitelyGeneratedGroup d group ->
      @RationalPolyhedralCone d cone ->
      @FinitelyGeneratedPeriodic d
        (fun value : ZVec d =>
          group value /\ cone (@zvec_to_qvec d value))
```

This is the proposition recorded by `lattice_gordan_contract`; neither the
interface definitions nor their quantification were changed.

## Mathematical connection

The implementation proves that `F_Z` preserves zero, natural scalar
multiplication, finite natural linear combinations, and generated-monoid
membership. It separately proves that a finitely generated group, the integer
points of a rational V-cone, and their intersection are periodic.

For an intersection point `x`, the group presentation yields integer
coefficients `z` with `x = F_Z(z)`. `F_Q_of_Z` and `QVecEq` transport cone
membership without treating numerical rational equality as Leibniz equality.
The pullback theorem places `z` in the coefficient-space cone, and the Hilbert
theorem expresses it as a nonnegative integer combination. Mapping that
combination through `F_Z` gives the final representation of `x`.

Conversely, every mapped coefficient-space generator belongs to both the
group and cone. Periodicity of the intersection then closes every natural
linear combination.
