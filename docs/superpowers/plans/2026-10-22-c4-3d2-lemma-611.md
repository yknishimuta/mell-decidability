# C4-3D2 Lemma 6.11 and finite slices implementation plan

**Goal:** Prove the two face-stripping invariants, finitary remainders, maximality of every nonempty current slice, finitary slices, and absence of candidates after the final stage.

**Architecture:** Add one module over the D1 recursion.  A compact `OrderedStrippingFaces` predicate packages exactly the existing finite face-list witness.  Candidate monotonicity and current-face elimination drive the rear-representation invariant; `extract_remainder_cylindric` drives remainder cylindricity.  Existing Lemma 6.8 (`finitary_qcylindric_sub`) and Lemma 6.10 provide finite bases without reimplementing WQO arguments.

**Scope:** C4-3D2 only.  Do not identify the final remainder with `excluded`, prove Theorem 6.1, or start C4-3E.

## Review focus

- `excluded` remains fixed at every stage.
- Candidate transport uses inclusion of remainders in the correct direction.
- A face `SameSet` to the processed face is eliminated as well.
- Maximality is proved only under nonemptiness of the current slice.
- Empty and nonempty slices are handled separately.
- Stage `length faces` yields the impossible interval `length faces <= i < length faces`.
